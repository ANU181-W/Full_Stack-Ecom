About Virat Kohli

Born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer and former captain of the Indian national cricket team. Known as "King Kohli," he is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of all time. He plays for Delhi in domestic cricket and Royal Challengers Bangalore in the Indian Premier League (IPL).

Kohli is an aggressive right-handed top-order batsman, renowned for his consistent scoring in all three formats of the game. He holds numerous batting records, including the most centuries in ODIs and the most runs in T20 Internationals, making him a true icon of the sport.

Key Achievements & Records

Most ODI Centuries

Virat Kohli holds the record for the most centuries in One Day Internationals, surpassing Sachin Tendulkar's long-standing record.

Fastest to 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, 11,000, 12,000 & 13,000 ODI runs

A testament to his incredible consistency, Kohli has shattered numerous records for reaching significant run milestones in ODIs.

ICC ODI Player of the Year (Multiple Times)

His dominance in the 50-over format has been recognized by the ICC on multiple occasions, winning the prestigious award.

Most Runs in a Single IPL Season

In 2016, Kohli scored an astonishing 973 runs in the IPL, including 4 centuries, a record that remains unbroken.

Only player to score 4000+ runs in T20Is

Kohli is the highest run-scorer in T20 Internationals, showcasing his mastery across all formats of the game.
